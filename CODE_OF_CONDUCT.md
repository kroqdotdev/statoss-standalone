# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, and discussions, agrees to these rules.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be clear. Say what you mean, and ask when you do not understand.
- Be patient. Maintainers and contributors work on this in their own time.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone was new once.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other behaviour that a reasonable person would find hostile or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository, or by opening an issue if the matter is not sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially. A maintainer may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules.

## Scope

These rules apply in every space the project uses, and whenever someone represents the project in public.
